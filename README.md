What is Animal Crossing: Wildworld?
------------------------------------

Animal Crossing: Wildworld is a life simulation video game developed by Nintendo. It was first released in 2002 for the GameCube and has since become one of the most popular and iconic games of all time.

In this browser-based version, you'll create your own character and customize their appearance, home, and lifestyle in the charming town of Wildworld. Your goal is to build a peaceful and fulfilling life with friends and animals, while exploring the game's vast open world.
